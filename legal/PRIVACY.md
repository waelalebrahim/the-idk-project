# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 12, 2026
**Service:** The I Don't Know Project ("the Service"), at theidkproject.ai
**Operated by:** Wael Alebrahim ("we", "us", "our")
**Contact:** contact@theidkproject.ai

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the
Service, how we use it, and your choices.

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you provide:**
- **Questions you ask.** The text of the questions you submit to the assistant.
- **Community submissions.** When you report a knowledge gap, flag an answer, or
  upvote, the content and the fact of that action. **Community submissions are
  public** and visible to other users.

**Information collected automatically:**
- **Technical and log data**, such as your IP address, browser type, device
  information, pages visited, and timestamps, collected by us and our hosting
  provider for security, abuse prevention, and operation of the Service.
- **Local storage.** We store small amounts of data in your browser (for
  example, to remember what you have upvoted). This stays on your device.

We do not intentionally collect special categories of personal data, and we ask
that you do not submit sensitive personal information in your questions or
community posts.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information to:
- operate, maintain, and improve the Service;
- generate answers to your questions;
- display and moderate the community board;
- protect the Service against abuse, fraud, and security threats;
- comply with legal obligations.

## 3. AI processing and third parties

To generate answers, the text of your question (together with the loaded source
material) is sent to our AI provider, **Anthropic**, for processing. Your use of
this feature is also subject to Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.

We also rely on:
- **Vercel** — hosting and request logs.

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell
your personal information.

## 4. Source content and external links

The Service answers from publicly available documents and links selected by the
operator, and may display links to external websites. We are not responsible for
the content or privacy practices of those external sources.

## 5. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as
required by law. Log data is retained for up to 30 days. Public community
submissions remain until removed by us or, where applicable, by the submitter.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or
restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to
data portability (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA).
To exercise any right, contact us at contact@theidkproject.ai. We will respond as
required by applicable law.

## 7. International users

The Service is operated from Kuwait and processed using providers that may be
located in other countries. By using the Service you understand your information
may be transferred and processed outside your country of residence.

## 8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect
their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact
us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information.
No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above shows the
latest version. Material changes will be indicated on this page.

## 11. Contact

Questions about this Policy: contact@theidkproject.ai.
